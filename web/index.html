How to read the figures

NameBio’s sample includes reported aftermarket transactions. Rolling windows overlap, so adding the month to the year would count sales twice. A few large sales can move an average, particularly when the sample is small.

CIRA reported 3,539,550 .CA domains under management at the end of its 2026 fiscal year. That registry figure measures the stock of registered domains. A registrar’s renewal fee and a seller’s asking price also answer different questions from the amount paid in a completed sale. CIRA’s FY26 report.
